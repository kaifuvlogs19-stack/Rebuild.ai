One repair journey. Three decisions made clearer.

When a device stops working, people face uncertainty: what failed, how much it could cost, and who can fix it. RepairBuild.Ai aims to connect these decisions in one guided experience.

Understand

What might be wrong?

Collect product details, symptoms, photos and error codes. Return likely causes and ask follow-up questions when evidence is incomplete.

Decide

What is the sensible fix?

Show safe troubleshooting steps, repair-versus-replace considerations and when professional help is necessary.

Connect

Where can I get help?

Compare compatible parts and connect with local technicians or repair businesses. Listings and rates must be verified.

Photo, voice and error-code input

Photo, model sticker, error code, symptom text or a voice note in Hindi and supported regional languages. Proposed.

AI-assisted diagnosis

Vision AI reads visible clues; a language model combines them with verified manuals to rank possible causes. Proposed.

From “it stopped working” to a clearer next step

The proposed journey reduces uncertainty instead of pretending every fault can be named from one photo.

  1. Share the problem

    Choose a device, or add a photo, voice description or error code.

  2. Add context

    Collect make, model, symptoms and relevant history.

  3. See possible faults

    AI-assisted ranking, with confidence and uncertainty explained.

  4. Check safety

    Decide whether to stop and call a professional.

  5. Get model-specific guidance

    Shown only where reliable information exists.

  6. Compare compatible parts

    Total cost, seller, availability, warranty and delivery where data exists.

  7. Fix or book

    Follow safe steps, or request a local technician.

  8. Feed back

    With consent, confirmed outcomes help improve future suggestions.

After the repair. With user consent, record the confirmed fault, actual part and cost, technician feedback and outcome. Feedback must be quality-checked; it does not automatically make the AI accurate.

Find the right part, not just the cheapest listing

Comparison is useful only when the part fits. The proposed flow confirms model and part number first, then compares total cost. Illustrative sample data

Sellers, prices, delivery and warranty are invented for this demo. Total = example price + example delivery charge. Compatibility labels assume a confirmed sample model. Real comparison needs verified catalogues and supplier integrations; “lowest” would apply only among checked listings.

Why technicians might join: qualified leads, less diagnosing from scratch, nearby visibility, fewer wasted visits. They will join only if jobs are worthwhile and fees are transparent.
How local shops could grow: be discovered when a user needs a compatible part. Ranking should weigh compatibility, total price, stock, service and reliability, not pay-to-win placement. Growth must be measured through real enquiries and sales.

Platform revenue is not the repair bill

Platform revenue = completed bookings × average commission + eligible parts or referral income + subscriptions, listings and B2B fees. Profit then subtracts AI and API costs, support, payment charges, refunds, verification, marketing and operations.

What happens after the pitch?

Build evidence in stages. No feature is promised before it works reliably.

Phase 1 · Validate

Understand real faults

Interview users and technicians, pick a narrow appliance scope, collect consented, verified fault examples.

Phase 2 · Proof of concept

Test diagnosis

Deck target: 5 appliances, 100 real faults, 80%+ correct first diagnosis. Define scoring and independent verification first.

Phase 3 · Web MVP

Demonstrate the flow

Web app for product details, guided troubleshooting, cost ranges and technician or parts enquiries. Unverified results labelled.

Phase 4 · Expand

Prove marketplace value

Onboard verified partners, measure completed repairs and unit economics, then consider mobile apps, sensors and more electronics.
